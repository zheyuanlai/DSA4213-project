# Case RP-09

## Research package (exactly what the systems received)

## Research question
Does adding Method M to existing white-box knowledge-distillation losses improve the instruction-following quality of distilled student models, consistently across KD losses and evaluation sets, and which of its components drive the change?

## Methods summary
Method M is a plug-in curriculum for knowledge distillation of LLMs. A difficulty measurer ranks training samples by fusing two rankings (ROUGE-L of the student's output against the reference, and the student's cross-entropy) with reciprocal rank fusion, then splits them into 4 subsets from easy to hard. A scheduler adds one subset per stage, while the distillation temperature rises linearly from 1 to 2 and, for off-policy losses, the weight of the supervised loss decays from 0.3 to 0. It is combined with existing KD losses (e.g., KLD, GKD, SRKL) and compared with the same losses without it, plus SFT and SeqKD baselines.

## Experimental setup
Teacher GPT-2 1.5B fine-tuned on databricks-dolly-15K (12.5K train / 1K validation / 0.5K test after filtering); students are GPT-2 120M models distilled with each method for the same number of iterations, hyperparameters chosen on validation ROUGE-L. Evaluation on five instruction-following sets (DollyEval, SelfInst, VicunaEval, S-NI, UnNI) with ROUGE-L. Responses are sampled at temperature 1 and scores are averaged over five generations per prompt with five random seeds; the number of independent training runs is not stated.

## Experiments and comparisons
- `exp1`: Plug-in evaluation across KD losses, GPT-2 family (T1)
  - comparison `exp1/cmp1`: Each KD loss with vs without Method M, per dataset and on average
  - comparison `exp1/cmp2`: Best distilled students vs the teacher
- `exp2`: Component ablation with KLD (T2)
  - comparison `exp2/cmp1`: Full Method M vs variants without the temperature schedule and/or the supervised-ratio schedule, and vs plain KLD
