# Case RP-12

## Research package (exactly what the systems received)

## Research question
When a pretrained LLM is given a different tokenizer without any further training, does initializing the unseen-token embeddings with Method M preserve the original model's benchmark performance better than simple and published initialization methods?

## Methods summary
Method M is a training-free way to swap a pretrained model's tokenizer for a donor model's tokenizer. Tokens shared by both vocabularies act as anchors. For each token that exists only in the donor vocabulary, Method M uses a greedy sparse-approximation algorithm to express the token's donor embedding as a sparse linear combination of k anchor embeddings in the donor model, then applies the same coefficients to the anchors' embeddings in the base model. Baselines: zero vectors (ZeroEmbed), the mean embedding (MeanEmbed), and published methods FOCUS, WECHSEL, CLPTransfer and ZETT, all applied zero-shot without their usual continued training.

## Experimental setup
Two settings: the Llama 3 vocabulary (about 128k tokens) transplanted into Mistral NeMo 12B (about 131k; about 54% overlap), and the Qwen 2.5 vocabulary (about 152k) transplanted into Llama 3 1B (about 128k; about 86% overlap). Evaluation with the EleutherAI LM Evaluation Harness: MMLU, ARC-Challenge, XNLI, AGIEval, Lambada (zero-shot), GSM8K (5-shot), Belebele, and WikiText bits per byte. The harness's bootstrapped standard errors are about 0.003 to 0.014 for the accuracy metrics (see T1 notes).

## Experiments and comparisons
- `exp1`: Llama 3 -> Mistral NeMo 12B transplant (vocabulary overlap about 54%) (T1)
  - comparison `exp1/cmp1`: Method M vs ZeroEmbed, MeanEmbed and FOCUS
  - comparison `exp1/cmp2`: Method M vs the original model (performance preservation), including GSM8K
  - comparison `exp1/cmp3`: Effect of the number of anchors k
- `exp2`: Qwen 2.5 -> Llama 3 1B transplant (vocabulary overlap about 86%) (T2)
  - comparison `exp2/cmp1`: Method M vs published zero-shot methods (CLPTransfer, FOCUS, WECHSEL, ZETT)
  - comparison `exp2/cmp2`: Method M vs simple ZeroEmbed / MeanEmbed initialization
