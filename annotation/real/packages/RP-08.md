## Research package (exactly what the systems received)

## Research question
Does refining LLM-generated utterances with Method M yield better training data for zero-shot intent detection in unseen domains than existing synthetic-data approaches, and how much of the gain depends on fine-tuning the rewriting model?

## Methods summary
Method M is a two-step data-augmentation approach for intent detection in domains with no labeled examples. First, an open LLM (Zephyr-7B-beta or Llama3-8B-Instruct) generates utterances for each unseen intent zero-shot. Second, a Flan-T5-large sequence-to-sequence rewriter, fine-tuned on seen domains to map LLM-generated utterances to real human utterances of the same intent (7 inputs to 1 output), rewrites them. The refined utterances train the intent classifier. Baselines: ZeroGen (directly generated utterances, including ChatGPT) and SuperGen (generated utterances selected by generation probability). The ablation uses the rewriter without fine-tuning (NFT).

## Experimental setup
Datasets: Clinc150 (10 domains, 150 intents) and SGD (20 domains, 46 intents). Each experiment randomly splits domains into seen and unseen (Clinc150: 5 unseen domains; SGD: 8 unseen), repeated over 5 different splits; results are averaged over these 5 runs. A DistilBERT classifier is trained on 100 (Clinc150) or 200 (SGD) synthetic utterances per unseen intent and evaluated on real user utterances from the unseen domains. Metric: accuracy.

## Experiments and comparisons
- `exp1`: Comparison with synthetic-data baselines (T1)
  - comparison `exp1/cmp1`: Method M vs ZeroGen and SuperGen with the same generator LLM (Zephyr, Llama3), SGD and Clinc150
  - comparison `exp1/cmp2`: Method M with open 7-8B generators vs ZeroGen with ChatGPT
- `exp2`: Ablation: fine-tuned vs non-fine-tuned rewriter (T2)
  - comparison `exp2/c1`: Fine-tuned vs NFT, 1x, SGD / Zephyr
  - comparison `exp2/c2`: Fine-tuned vs NFT, 1x, SGD / Llama3
  - comparison `exp2/c3`: Fine-tuned vs NFT, 1x, Clinc150 / Zephyr
  - comparison `exp2/c4`: Fine-tuned vs NFT, 1x, Clinc150 / Llama3
