# Case RP-04

## Research package (exactly what the systems received)

## Research question
Do the Amharic-specific Model M encoders retrieve passages more effectively than general-purpose multilingual embedding models, and does any advantage carry over to a different Amharic test collection?

## Methods summary
Model M is a family of three Amharic dense bi-encoder retrievers (40M, 42M and 110M parameters) built on Amharic BERT/RoBERTa encoders with a 512-token context. They are trained on Amharic query-passage pairs with a multiple-negatives ranking loss (4 epochs, sentence-transformers). Baselines are general-purpose multilingual embedding models used without Amharic fine-tuning: gte-modernbert-base, gte-multilingual-base, multilingual-e5-large-instruct and snowflake-arctic-embed-l-v2.0.

## Experimental setup
Main data: an Amharic passage retrieval set built from an Amharic news classification corpus, using each headline as a query and its article body as the only relevant passage (about 45K pairs after de-duplication; 10% held out, stratified by news category). Model M is trained on the remaining 90%. Metrics: MRR@k, NDCG@k (binary relevance) and Recall@k. Generalization: zero-shot evaluation on 2AIRTC, a separate TREC-style Amharic collection with manually assessed topics. Significance for the main table uses a paired t-test; no seeds or variance are reported.

## Experiments and comparisons
- `exp1`: In-domain Amharic passage retrieval (T1)
  - comparison `exp1/cmp1`: Model M-110M vs the strongest multilingual baseline on MRR@10, NDCG@10 and Recall@10/50/100
  - comparison `exp1/cmp2`: Smaller Model M variants (40M, 42M) vs multilingual baselines
- `exp2`: Zero-shot generalization to the 2AIRTC collection (T2)
  - comparison `exp2/cmp1`: Model M variants vs multilingual baselines on 2AIRTC
