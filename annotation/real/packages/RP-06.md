# Case RP-06

## Research package (exactly what the systems received)

## Research question
Does training a translation model with Method M improve translation quality over supervised fine-tuning and reinforcement-learning baselines, and is the improvement consistent across low- and high-resource target languages and metrics?

## Methods summary
Method M fine-tunes a neural MT model with two losses at once: cross-entropy on labeled sentence pairs and an 'energy' loss given by the negative score of a reference-free quality estimator (COMET-KIWI) on the model's own sampled translations of unlabeled source sentences; gradients pass through the estimator into the MT model via a straight-through estimator. Method M-static keeps the estimator fixed; Method M-dynamic also updates it contrastively to prefer gold over sampled translations. Two data techniques are added: filtering labeled pairs by estimator score (FILTER) and pairing each labeled batch with similar unlabeled sources (NN). RL baselines (REINFORCE, PPO) use the same estimator score as reward.

## Experimental setup
MT model: mBART-50 (about 610M parameters, monolingual pretraining only). Data: IWSLT2017 English-German/Chinese (50K pairs sampled) and ML50 English-Bengali, -Azerbaijani, -Mongolian, -Marathi, -Kazakh; one fifth of the pairs are used as labeled data and all source sentences as unlabeled data. Training: 10 epochs with early stopping on validation COMET-KIWI. Metrics: SacreBLEU, COMET-KIWI (reference-free), COMET (reference-based) and XCOMET. Main-table numbers are means over three seeds.

## Experiments and comparisons
- `exp1`: Main comparison with supervised fine-tuning on seven language pairs (T1)
  - comparison `exp1/cmp1`: Method M (static and dynamic) vs supervised fine-tuning, low-resource pairs (BN, AZ, MN, MR, KA)
  - comparison `exp1/cmp2`: Method M (static and dynamic) vs supervised fine-tuning, high-resource pairs (DE, ZH)
- `exp2`: Comparison with RL baselines, averaged over four languages (T2)
  - comparison `exp2/cmp1`: Method M vs REINFORCE and PPO on BLEU, COMET-KIWI, COMET, XCOMET
  - comparison `exp2/cmp2`: Method M-dynamic with vs without the FILTER/NN data techniques
