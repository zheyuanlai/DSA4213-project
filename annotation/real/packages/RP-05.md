## Research package (exactly what the systems received)

## Research question
Does Method M produce news summaries that follow a requested discourse structure more closely than prompting-based LLM baselines, and does this come at a cost in content accuracy?

## Methods summary
The task is to summarize a news article into a sequence of sentences that each play a requested discourse role (e.g., introductory element, contextual detail, source attribution). A sentence-level discourse classifier, trained on Dataset D and reported at over 90% validation accuracy, guides generation. Method M (beam variant) writes the summary one target role at a time: LLaMa-3-8B proposes candidate sentences (beam size 16) and the classifier keeps those that best match the target role. Method M (edit variant) drafts a full summary and repeatedly regenerates sentences whose predicted role is wrong.

## Experimental setup
Data: Dataset D, news articles paired with social-media and newsletter summaries labelled with sentence-level discourse roles; target role sequences are sampled randomly per generation. Baselines: DeepSeek-V3, Claude-3.5-Sonnet, GPT-4o, Qwen-2.5 and LLaMa-3-8B prompted directly (the paper states that vanilla generation keeps the best of 16 samples according to the same classifier), plus LLaMa-3-8B fine-tuned with PEFT on Dataset D. Metrics: ROUGE-L, FactCC and AlignScore for content; Match Score, Levenshtein distance and LCS between predicted and target role sequences, using either the automatic classifier or two human annotators.

## Experiments and comparisons
- `exp1`: Structure-controlled summarization: content accuracy and structural adherence (T1)
  - comparison `exp1/cmp1`: Method M (beam variant) vs proprietary and open-source prompting baselines on structural metrics (automatic and human)
  - comparison `exp1/cmp2`: Method M (beam variant) vs baselines on content-accuracy metrics (ROUGE-L, FactCC, AlignScore)
  - comparison `exp1/cmp3`: Method M edit variant vs beam variant, and fine-tuned LLaMa-3-8B vs vanilla LLaMa-3-8B
