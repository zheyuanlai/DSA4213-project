## Research package (exactly what the systems received)

## Research question
Does training a RAG reranker with Method M select relevant documents and support answer generation better than existing LLM-supervised reranker training objectives, consistently across datasets and reranker backbones?

## Methods summary
Method M trains the reranker of a retrieval-augmented QA system end to end from the reader's answer loss, without document relevance labels. Reranking is viewed as a document-level attention mask over independently encoded candidates; the hard top-k mask is relaxed into a stochastic, differentiable top-k selection so that the reader's language-modeling loss on the gold answer back-propagates into the reranker scores. Baselines are four LLM-supervised reranker training objectives: EMDR, PDist, LOOP and ADist. The reader's parameters are kept fixed in the comparisons.

## Experimental setup
Datasets: multi-hop HotpotQA, Musique and 2WikiHop in the distractor setting (gold documents are among the candidates; Musique padded with random candidates to 20 per query). Reader: FiD-Large. Reranker backbones: RankT5-Base and BGE-Base-Reranker. 'Reranker setting' measures whether the reranker ranks relevant documents in its top 5 for test questions; 'Generator setting' measures exact match of the generated answers. One run per configuration; no variance estimates or significance tests are reported.

## Experiments and comparisons
- `exp1`: Test-time document reranking quality on three multi-hop QA datasets with two reranker backbones (T1)
  - comparison `exp1/cmp1`: Method M vs the best LLM-supervised baseline per dataset, RankT5 backbone
  - comparison `exp1/cmp2`: Method M vs the best LLM-supervised baseline per dataset, BGE-Base backbone
- `exp2`: End-to-end answer quality (T2)
  - comparison `exp2/cmp1`: Method M vs baselines in exact match across datasets and backbones
