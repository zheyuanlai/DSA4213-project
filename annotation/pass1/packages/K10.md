# Case K10

## Research package (exactly what the systems received)

## Research question
How does Glimmer compare with Trellis-RE across the three relation-extraction test sets, and what does the ablation show about the evidence-gating module?

## Methods summary
Glimmer is a document-level relation extractor that adds an evidence-gating module, which down-weights sentences judged irrelevant to an entity pair, on top of a Trellis-RE-style encoder. Trellis-RE is the main baseline. 'Glimmer w/o evidence gating' removes only the gating module. All systems are trained on Hollin-DocRE only.

## Experimental setup
Hollin-DocRE is the in-domain test set; Saltmarsh-Bio (biomedical) and Wistmoor-News (news) are out-of-domain test sets not used for training or tuning. Metric: relation F1 (%, higher is better), mean over 3 seeds; the seed standard deviation is at most 0.4 F1 for every entry.

## Experiments and comparisons
- `exp1`: Main comparison and ablation on three test sets
  - comparison `exp1/cmp1`: Glimmer (full) vs Trellis-RE: F1 per test set
  - comparison `exp1/cmp2`: Glimmer (full) vs Glimmer w/o evidence gating: F1 per test set (ablation)
