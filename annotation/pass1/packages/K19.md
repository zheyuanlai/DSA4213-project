## Research package (exactly what the systems received)

## Research question
How do Thimble's long-tail recommendation results on Garnet-Retail relate to what prior work reports for popularity-debiased negative sampling?

## Methods summary
Thimble is a graph-based recommender built on Cobble-GNN. It adopts popularity-debiased negative sampling (Sorensen-Nakata et al., 2022), which is widely credited with roughly 20% relative gains in long-tail Recall@20, and adds a session-aware item encoder. 'Cobble-GNN + debiased sampling' applies only the sampling change.

## Experimental setup
Garnet-Retail (Imre & Castellanos, 2020) test split. Long-tail items are the 80% least-interacted items in training. Recall@20 is computed over all target items (overall) and over long-tail target items only (long-tail); higher is better. Values are means over 3 runs. Training time was not measured.

## Experiments and comparisons
- `exp1`: Recommendation accuracy on Garnet-Retail test
  - comparison `exp1/cmp1`: Thimble vs Cobble-GNN: overall and long-tail Recall@20
  - comparison `exp1/cmp2`: Cobble-GNN + debiased sampling vs Cobble-GNN: long-tail Recall@20
