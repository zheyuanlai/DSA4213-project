## Research package (exactly what the systems received)

## Research question
Is Marrowfield-ASR robust to additive background noise?

## Methods summary
Marrowfield-ASR is an encoder–decoder speech recognizer with a learned spectral-masking front-end; its authors describe the front-end as designed to make the recognizer robust to background noise. Ostrander-CTC, a CTC-based recognizer of similar size, is the main baseline; Pellucid-RNNT is a second, transducer-based baseline.

## Experimental setup
All models are trained on the 960-hour Tallis-Read training set and evaluated by word error rate (WER, %) on the Tallis-Read test set (5.4 hours of studio-quality read speech). Each model is trained with 3 seeds and WER is averaged over seeds. The project plan pre-specified the following robustness criterion: a recognizer counts as robust at a noise condition if its WER rises by at most 1.5 absolute points above its WER on the original recordings.
