## Research package (exactly what the systems received)

## Research question
How does Marrowline-int8 compare with Selk-MT in translation quality and serving latency?

## Methods summary
Marrowline is our translation model compressed with mixed-precision quantization; Marrowline-int8 and Marrowline-int4 use 8-bit and 4-bit weights, respectively. Selk-MT is the uncompressed fp16 production model that Marrowline is intended to replace.

## Experimental setup
Quality is chrF on the Ombria-News test set (2,500 sentences; higher is better). Latency is per-request wall-clock time measured on one Pellmar P2 inference card while replaying a production trace of 20,000 requests at 40 requests/s; p50 and p99 are the median and 99th-percentile request latencies.

## Experiments and comparisons
- `exp1`: Quality and latency of compressed vs production model
  - comparison `exp1/cmp1`: Marrowline-int8 vs Selk-MT: chrF, p50 and p99 latency
  - comparison `exp1/cmp2`: Marrowline-int4 vs Selk-MT: chrF, p50 and p99 latency
