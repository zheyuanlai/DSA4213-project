## Research package (exactly what the systems received)

## Research question
Is Stellate-Net robust to Gaussian blur in its input images?

## Methods summary
Stellate-Net is a convolutional land-cover classifier trained with frequency-mixing augmentation. Lattice-CNN is a standard convolutional classifier of comparable size (23M vs 24M parameters) trained on the same data with the same schedule.

## Experimental setup
Both models are trained on the Ostmark-Aerial training split (24,000 images, 10 land-cover classes) and evaluated by top-1 accuracy on its 6,000-image test split; accuracies are means over 3 seeds. Blur study: test images are blurred with a Gaussian kernel at severity 1 (σ = 1 px), severity 3 (σ = 2 px) and severity 5 (σ = 3 px); blurred images are never used for training or tuning, and the frequency-mixing augmentation does not include blur. Pre-specified robustness criterion: a model counts as robust at a severity if its top-1 accuracy drops by at most 3.0 points from its clean accuracy.

## Experiments and comparisons
- `exp1`: Clean and blurred evaluation on the Ostmark-Aerial test split
  - comparison `exp1/cmp1`: Stellate-Net: accuracy drop from clean at each blur severity
  - comparison `exp1/cmp2`: Lattice-CNN: accuracy drop from clean at each blur severity
