## Research question
How do Corbel's zero-shot retrieval gains compare with the improvements previously reported for denoised hard-negative mining?

## Methods summary
Corbel is a dense bi-encoder retriever trained with denoised hard-negative mining (Hallberg & Osei, 2023), which is commonly cited as improving zero-shot nDCG@10 by about 5 points across diverse domains; Corbel adds query-side length normalization. Dovetail-DR is the same bi-encoder trained with standard in-batch negatives. Lexi-Sparse is a lexical reference retriever.

## Experimental setup
Both dense retrievers are trained on the same 480k query–passage pairs and evaluated zero-shot (no in-domain training) on four held-out collections described by Nakashima-Reyes & Thorne (2021): Brackwater-News, Felloe-Fin, Moorstone-Bio and Lintel-Law. Metric: nDCG@10 (×100; higher is better); single training run per system.
