# Case K31

## Research package (exactly what the systems received)

## Research question
Does Gannet's advantage over Ledger-RNN generalize beyond the map families it was trained on?

## Methods summary
Gannet is a navigation agent with a map-structured episodic memory; Ledger-RNN is a recurrent agent with the same parameter count (6.2M). Both are trained with the same on-policy actor-critic algorithm and a 200M-step budget per seed.

## Experimental setup
Agents are trained and tuned on procedurally generated layouts from three map families of the Mosswick navigation suite: Warehouse, Office and Cavern. Evaluation uses 500 new layouts per family, produced by the same three family generators with random seeds never used during training or tuning (unseen layouts). An episode succeeds if the agent reaches the goal within 500 steps. Metrics: success rate (%) and mean episode length of successful episodes (steps; lower is better), each averaged over 5 training seeds.

## Experiments and comparisons
- `exp1`: Evaluation on unseen layouts from the training map families (Warehouse, Office, Cavern)
  - comparison `exp1/cmp1`: Gannet vs Ledger-RNN, success rate, per training family
