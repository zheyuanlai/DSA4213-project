## Research question
Is Sablewing a more efficient sampler than Ordell-DDS for 256×256 face generation, and what does it cost in sample quality?

## Methods summary
Sablewing distils the Ordell-DDS denoiser into a student network that generates an image in 8 denoising steps (8 network function evaluations, NFE). The student is slightly wider than the teacher, so each of its evaluations costs somewhat more compute. Ordell-DDS is the 50-step teacher sampler; Ordell-DDS-10 runs the same sampler truncated to 10 steps.

## Experimental setup
Each sampler generates 30,000 images at 256×256 resolution for Halvern-Faces; FID is computed against the full Halvern-Faces reference set (lower is better). NFE counts denoiser evaluations per image, and TFLOPs per image are computed analytically from the network architecture and NFE. All models use fp16 weights.

## Experiments and comparisons
- `exp1`: Sampler comparison on Halvern-Faces 256×256
  - comparison `exp1/cmp1`: Sablewing vs Ordell-DDS (50 steps): sampling cost and FID
  - comparison `exp1/cmp2`: Sablewing vs Ordell-DDS-10: sampling cost and FID
