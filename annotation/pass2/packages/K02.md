## Research question
Does Calloway-Sum's advantage over Pellam-Sum carry over to text domains other than news?

## Methods summary
Calloway-Sum is an abstractive summarizer that first plans a sequence of salient entities and then generates the summary conditioned on that plan. Pellam-Sum is an abstractive summarizer of the same size without the planning stage. Lead-3 (the first three sentences of the input) is a simple extractive reference.

## Experimental setup
Calloway-Sum and Pellam-Sum are trained, tuned and checkpoint-selected using only the Ravenmoor News corpus (210,000 article–summary pairs). In-domain evaluation uses the Ravenmoor News test split (2,000 articles). Held-out domains, never used for training, tuning or model selection: Lindqvist-Sci (scientific articles to abstracts), Corbin-Legal (court opinions to case summaries), Ashdown-Meet (meeting transcripts to minutes) and Gable-Reviews (product-review threads to digests), 1,000 test documents each. Metric: ROUGE-L F1 (%), mean over 3 seeds; inputs longer than the 4,096-token context are truncated identically for both models.

## Experiments and comparisons
- `exp1`: In-domain evaluation (Ravenmoor News test split)
  - comparison `exp1/cmp1`: Calloway-Sum vs Pellam-Sum, ROUGE-L, in-domain
- `exp2`: Held-out-domain evaluation
  - comparison `exp2/cmp2`: Calloway-Sum vs Pellam-Sum, ROUGE-L, per held-out domain
