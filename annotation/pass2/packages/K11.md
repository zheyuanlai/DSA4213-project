## Research question
Does Halyard outperform Tidewell across the forecasting benchmark suite?

## Methods summary
Halyard is a patch-based transformer forecaster with per-series normalization. Tidewell, a seasonal-trend decomposition forecaster, is the strongest baseline in our study; seasonal-naive (repeating the value from one season earlier) is a reference.

## Experimental setup
The suite contains five multivariate forecasting datasets: Kelso-Grid (electricity load), Marrick-Traffic (road occupancy), Doveton-Weather (meteorological sensors), Ashby-Retail (store sales) and Pell-Solar (photovoltaic output). All methods forecast 96 steps ahead from a 336-step input window, use chronological 70/10/20 splits and receive the same tuning budget on the validation split. Metric: test MAE on z-normalized series (lower is better), mean over 3 seeds, reported to three decimals.

## Experiments and comparisons
- `exp1`: Five-dataset forecasting suite (horizon 96)
  - comparison `exp1/cmp1`: Halyard vs Tidewell, test MAE, per dataset
  - comparison `exp1/cmp2`: Halyard vs seasonal-naive, test MAE, per dataset
