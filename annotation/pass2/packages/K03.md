## Research package (exactly what the systems received)

## Research question
How does Skua compare with Plover-PG on the held-out Mirefield levels?

## Methods summary
Skua adds a learned novelty bonus to the Plover-PG policy-gradient agent. 'Plover-PG + Tally bonus' adds a simple count-based bonus instead and is included as a further reference. All agents are trained for 25M environment steps on 500 Mirefield training levels.

## Experimental setup
Evaluation uses 200 held-out Mirefield levels never seen in training. Before running the experiments we pre-registered held-out success rate (% of levels solved) as the primary endpoint and mean normalized return on the held-out levels (0–100) as a secondary endpoint. Each agent was trained with the same 10 random seeds, and Skua is compared with Plover-PG using two-sided paired t-tests over seeds (α = 0.05).

## Experiments and comparisons
- `exp1`: Held-out evaluation on 200 Mirefield levels
  - comparison `exp1/cmp1`: Skua vs Plover-PG: normalized return
  - comparison `exp1/cmp2`: Skua vs Plover-PG: success rate

## Results tables

## Statistical tests
- `S1` for comparison `exp1/cmp1`: paired t-test; two-sided; paired; n=10 random seeds; t=1.18 (df=9); p = 0.27; 95% CI [-0.84, 2.66] for mean paired difference in normalized return (Skua − Plover-PG); alpha=0.05; note: Pre-registered secondary endpoint.
- `S2` for comparison `exp1/cmp2`: paired t-test; two-sided; paired; n=10 random seeds; t=4.05 (df=9); p = 0.0029; 95% CI [1.82, 6.44] for mean paired difference in success rate (Skua − Plover-PG), percentage points; alpha=0.05; note: Pre-registered primary endpoint.
