## Research question
How does Bramblewood compare with Larkspur-Seq in NDCG@10 on the Brightcart-Grocery dataset?

## Methods summary
Bramblewood is a session-based recommender that builds an item-transition graph for each session and scores candidate items with a gated graph encoder. Larkspur-Seq, a self-attentive sequential recommender, is the strongest baseline in our study; Most-Popular, which recommends globally popular items, is a non-personalized reference.

## Experimental setup
Models are trained on the first 26 days of Brightcart-Grocery logs (1.2M sessions) and evaluated on sessions from the final 4 days (61,400 sessions) with leave-last-item-out next-item prediction over the full item catalogue. Hyperparameters were tuned on a validation split from the training period. Bramblewood and Larkspur-Seq were each trained with 10 random seeds (seeds 0–9); seed i fixes initialization and mini-batch order for both models, so their seed-level scores are paired. Most-Popular is deterministic.

## Experiments and comparisons
- `exp1`: Next-item prediction on the Brightcart-Grocery test period
  - comparison `exp1/cmp1`: Bramblewood vs Larkspur-Seq, NDCG@10
  - comparison `exp1/cmp2`: Bramblewood vs Most-Popular, NDCG@10
