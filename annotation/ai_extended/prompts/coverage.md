# Task: finding coverage (prompt ext-coverage)

Below is a research package, a checklist of findings that a complete Results section should report, and several passages written from the package. For **every passage and every checklist item**, decide whether the passage reports the finding.

* `CORRECT`: the passage states the finding with the correct direction, entities, dataset/metric and scope. Exact numbers are not required (they are scored separately), but a stated number or qualifier must not change what the finding says. A finding stated in different words, or split across sentences, counts.
* `INCORRECT`: the passage addresses the finding but misreports it: wrong direction or entity, wrong scope, or overstated/understated so that the finding as communicated is wrong (e.g. presents a null result as an improvement, or a tested significant difference as inconclusive).
* `ABSENT`: the passage does not report the finding.

Judge only against the package. Give a justification of at most 25 words for every decision (quote the deciding words of the passage when the status is CORRECT or INCORRECT).
