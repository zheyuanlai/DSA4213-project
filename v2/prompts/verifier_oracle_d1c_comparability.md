## Comparisons

When the sentence compares results (for example, that one method is better, faster or more robust than another), the comparison is only as strong as the comparability of those results. If the package indicates that the compared results were obtained under different conditions (for example a different dataset, metric or measurement setup), the strongest supported statement must reflect that limitation; do not treat such a comparison as established. Do not assume that conditions differ when the package does not say so.
